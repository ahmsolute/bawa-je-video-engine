Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Bawa Je Video Engine ("the Application"), operated by Teram Agency ("we", "us"), accesses and handles data when connecting to Google Drive.

What we access

The Application requests read/write access to a designated Google Drive folder solely to retrieve source footage and project assets, and to store rendered video output and related working files.

How we use it

Data obtained through the Google Drive API is used exclusively to operate the Application's internal video-production pipeline (content retrieval, automated editing, rendering, and delivery of finished media). We do not sell this data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Limited Use disclosure

Bawa Je Video Engine's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Data sharing

We do not share, transfer, or disclose Google user data to third parties, except where required to provide the Application's core functionality (e.g. the rendering pipeline operating on infrastructure we directly control) or where required by law.

Data retention

Files remain in the connected Google Drive account under the user's own control. Cached copies held temporarily on our rendering server are purged after processing completes.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time via Google Account → Security → Third-party access.
